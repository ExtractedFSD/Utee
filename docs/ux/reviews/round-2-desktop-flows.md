# Round 2 review: desktop task flows (1440px)

Material: `docs/ux/screenshots/round-2/flows/desktop/` (77 full-page captures across the log, logEmpty, today, result, investigation, taking, share, share-clinician, una, settings, slow and signin flows). Reviewed cold against `docs/ux/rubric.md` and `docs/ux/product-map.md`. Mobile material was not supplied, so heading 3 is not scored.

## 1. Scores

| Heading | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Navigation and findability | 6 | Breadcrumbs, one h1 per page and a persistent Passport pill bar are mostly in place, but the Log a UTI page and its open-UTI guard drop the Passport navigation, the episode's change-date and delete actions hide behind an unlabelled "…" menu, and several links ("Details", "Manage", "Open that UTI", "Go back") do not say where they go. |
| 2. Clarity of wording | 7 | Copy is short, direct, NHS-voiced and free of blame on almost every screen; it slips on jargon that reaches the patient ("USS KUB", "Cystoscopy", "Laparoscopy", "eGFR", ">10^5", "FHIR"), full stops in hints, "Tap" on a mouse-driven desktop, and a few over-long consent sentences. |
| 3. Mobile layout | not scored | No phone captures in this round's material. |
| 4. Desktop layout | 7 | Dialogs are a sensible 640px and pages have a maximum width, but Passport pages use three different content widths, inline confirmations inside dialogs are clipped at the dialog edge, the Add something dialog jumps vertically as it resizes, and the five faces are spread across an 1100px hero. |
| 5. Forms and data entry | 5 | Chips, pre-filled edits and "I don't know the date" are good, but single-choice questions come pre-selected (Today, Still going, Standard culture, Normal, 7 days), three forms rely on a native date picker as the only way to give a date, "(optional)" is missing on several fields, two fields use placeholders as labels, the wrong share code is cleared on error, and the consent form gives no feedback when submitted unticked. |
| 6. Feedback and states | 5 | Removals, deletion and logging a UTI are confirmed with clear banners, but there is no error summary anywhere, the result dialog's errors sit far from their fields, the consent page shows nothing on an empty submit, the Today note says "Saved ✓" while the Save button looks disabled, consent withdrawal is not confirmed, adds produce no message and leave identical duplicate cards, and the investigation remove confirmation is cut off inside its dialog. |
| 7. First-time experience | 6 | Sign-in is passwordless, the consent page is plain and untouched, and guided setup offers "Prefer a form?" and "Skip for now"; but a returning user who re-consents is sent through guided setup again, the consent page has no route back, and no help or contact route appears on sign-in or consent. |
| 8. Accessibility | 6 | Focus rings are visible on inputs, S/I/R pills carry letters not just colour, and headings are structured; but muted white-on-pink hero text, pale "disabled" buttons, grey S/I/R toggles, colour-only "Feeling" squares, icon-only "…" buttons and 16px native checkboxes all look likely to fail contrast or target rules and need measuring. |
| 9. Trust and tone | 7 | Tone is calm and honest ("This is your record, not advice", "not opened yet", explicit share consent, "Code removed. Anyone using it has lost access."), but an investigation defaults to "Normal" and that default reaches the clinician view, the clinician summary says "Nothing recorded for: medical history" above a recorded allergy, and a shop nudge sits at the foot of the health record. |
| 10. Consistency | 5 | The same job is done four different ways: Save versus Done versus autosave, a named edit dialog versus a bare "Edit", a visible Remove versus none, four different removal messages, six date formats, "I feel better" as a link on Today and a button on the episode page, and three content widths. |

## 2. Problems found

Flow step names follow the file names in `round-2/flows/desktop/`. All at 1440px.

### Log a UTI (log-01 to log-14)

1. log-01, log-06, logEmpty-01, desktop. The Log a UTI page and the "You have a UTI open" guard page show only the portal top bar; the Passport pill bar (Today, History, Record, Share, Una) is missing, although it is present on every other Passport page. Breaks 1.4. Major. Fix: render the Passport navigation on the Log page and guard page as on Today.
2. log-01, logEmpty-02, desktop. "When did it start?" has "Today" pre-selected with a tick, so a tired user can save a wrong start date without choosing one. Breaks 5.6 (7.5 allows a visible default but the tick makes it read as an answer). Minor. Fix: show "Today" as a default without the selected style, or make it a true unselected choice and let "Save" default to today only if nothing is chosen.
3. log-02, desktop. "Is it still going?" appears with "Still going" pre-selected. Breaks 5.6. Minor. Fix: present both options unselected; if nothing is chosen, treat as still going and say so in the hint.
4. log-01, desktop. Hint "Choose all that apply." ends with a full stop; other hints ("Tap each one on the report.") do too. Breaks 2.8. Minor. Fix: remove trailing full stops from one-phrase hints.
5. log-02 to log-04, desktop. Each chosen symptom reveals a 1 to 10 scale labelled "Tap to rate". "Tap" is phone wording on a mouse-driven page, and the rating is never marked "(optional)" though saving without it is allowed. Breaks 2.8, 5.3, 4.10. Minor. Fix: "Rate it (optional)" and "1 mild, 10 worst" as a hint.
6. log-03, log-04, desktop. The long "Other" description ("A dull ache low down that comes and goes…") becomes the rating heading and pushes "Tap to rate" into a two-line wrap on the right. Breaks 4.4. Minor. Fix: truncate the rating heading to one line with the full text as a tooltip or on the next line, and keep the helper beneath.
7. log-03, desktop. "Describe the other symptom" is a single-line input about 450px wide; long entries scroll out of view while typing. Breaks 4.3. Minor. Fix: use a two-line textarea the width of the card.
8. log-05, desktop. The Today hero spreads five faces across the full 1100px card with about 200px between them; the eye has to travel the whole width to compare them. Breaks 4.2, 4.4. Minor. Fix: group the faces in a 480px row aligned left under the question.
9. log-05, today-01, desktop. "Add a note" and "I feel better" are underlined links but perform actions (open a field, end the UTI); on the episode page "I feel better" is a primary button. Breaks 1.6, 10.1. Minor. Fix: style both as secondary buttons on Today and keep the same label and style on the episode page.
10. log-05, today-01, desktop. "Details" (top right of the hero) and "Manage" (What I'm taking) do not say where they go. Breaks 1.5. Minor. Fix: "This UTI" and "Edit what I'm taking".
11. log-05, desktop. At a glance shows "Last investigation USS KUB · 11 May": a clinical abbreviation and a date with no year while every other date on the page has one. Breaks 2.2, 2.9, 10.4. Minor. Fix: "Kidney and bladder ultrasound (USS KUB) · 11 May 2026".
12. log-07, log-14, slow-03, desktop. History cards show "Feeling" as a row of coloured squares with no numbers, labels or legend, so the information is colour only. Breaks 8.3. Minor. Fix: add a text summary ("mostly bad, improving") or a tooltip and legend, and give each square an accessible label.
13. log-07, log-14, desktop. "Tap a UTI to open it." under the list uses phone wording, and the cards have no visible link affordance. Breaks 2.x phrasing, 1.5. Minor. Fix: "Open a UTI to see or change it" and a chevron or "Open" link on each card.
14. log-08, desktop. The episode page repeats the date: eyebrow "DAY 2 · STARTED 23 NOVEMBER 2026" then h1 "Since 23 November 2026". Breaks 1.1. Minor. Fix: h1 "UTI from 23 November 2026", eyebrow "Day 2 · open".
15. log-08, log-11, desktop. "Change start date" and "Delete this UTI" are hidden behind an unlabelled "…" icon button, the only way to fix a wrong date (top task 10). Breaks 1.9, 8.10. Major. Fix: show "Change start date" as a text link next to the date and "Delete this UTI" at the foot of the page; give any remaining icon button an accessible name.
16. log-08, desktop. "Notes None" sits beside "Possible triggers None recorded", "Treatment None recorded", "Tests None recorded". Breaks 10.5. Minor. Fix: "None recorded" throughout, or "None yet" throughout.
17. log-09, desktop. While the "When did you start feeling better?" panel is open with its own "SAVE" and "CANCEL", the page's "I FEEL BETTER" primary button is still shown below, so two primary buttons compete. Breaks 10.2, 4.5. Minor. Fix: hide or disable-with-reason the page button while the panel is open.
18. log-09, log-12, desktop. The end-date panel uses "SAVE" and the change-start-date panel uses "DONE" for the same kind of inline edit, and neither says what is saved. Breaks 10.1, 2.7. Minor. Fix: "Save end date" and "Save start date".
19. log-10, desktop. After ending the UTI there is no confirmation message; the eyebrow silently changes to "ENDED TUE 24 NOV · 2 DAYS", a third date format ("Tue 24 Nov") on one page. Breaks 6.8, 10.4, 2.5. Minor. Fix: banner "UTI ended on 24 November 2026" and eyebrow "Ended 24 November 2026 · 2 days".
20. log-10, desktop. Ending a UTI (top task 4: "end a UTI and say what worked") never asks about the treatment or whether it helped; the Treatment row is left "None recorded". Breaks 5.8 (the app should offer what it knows is next). Minor. Fix: after "I feel better", offer "Add the antibiotic you took (optional)".
21. log-13, desktop. The delete confirmation button is "YES, DELETE IT" while every other remove confirmation uses "REMOVE"; the "Yes," prefix is the pattern the rubric warns against. Breaks 2.7, 10.1. Minor. Fix: "Delete this UTI".

## 3. Could not be judged from the material

- Native date inputs show "mm/dd/yyyy" and "04/03/2026" because of the automation browser's locale; the real order shown to a UK user, and whether day-month-year text entry exists anywhere, need checking by hand.
- Whether the letter-spaced capitals on buttons, nav and pills are CSS `text-transform` over sentence-case text (acceptable under 2.6) or literal capitals in the markup.
- Text and non-text contrast ratios (hero muted text on the gradient, pale disabled buttons, grey S/I/R letters, hint text, pill text) need measuring; the screenshots only suggest they are marginal.
- Keyboard operation, focus order, focus visibility on chips and pills, whether dialogs trap focus and return it, and whether the "…" menus and S/I/R toggles have accessible names.
- Screen-reader announcements for "Saved ✓", "Result removed.", loading skeletons and the chat replies.
- Whether the Today note autosaves (which would explain the permanent "Saved ✓") or whether the pale "SAVE NOTE" button is genuinely disabled; the captures cannot separate the two.
- Whether the result Edit dialog has a "Remove" control below the visible area (result-08) or whether removal happened without a confirmation.
- Whether the clinician's wrong code was cleared by the app or by the automation before the capture (share-clinician-01).
- Whether Settings toggles show any saved feedback, and whether "Type DELETE to confirm" accepts lower case.
- Whether the "Save with no symptoms yet?" card is within the first viewport after pressing the sticky Save button on a typical 900px-tall desktop window.
- Browser Back behaviour after ending, deleting or re-dating a UTI, and after closing a dialog.
- Loading behaviour beyond three seconds (the page had loaded by the 3-second capture), and what shows on a failed request or an expired session.
- Whether the "Earlier" date option on the Log form and the end-date panel reveals a typed-date alternative.
- Whether symptom severity ratings entered on the Log form are shown anywhere afterwards (the episode page lists symptoms without the "7 of 10" rating).
- The Everything and Day by day views of History, the About you tab, the Past codes expander, the PDF, and the printable copy were not exercised in these flows.
