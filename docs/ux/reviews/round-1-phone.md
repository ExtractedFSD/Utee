# UX review, round 1, phone (390px)

Independent review from screenshots only: `docs/ux/screenshots/round-0/phone/` (32 static screens) and `docs/ux/screenshots/round-1/flows/phone/` (73 flow steps), scored against `docs/ux/rubric.md` with `docs/ux/product-map.md` as the map. No source code, no live site. Phone screen = top 844px of each capture; fixed bars appearing mid-image are treated as fixed to the top or bottom of the viewport.

Severity follows the rubric: critical = cannot complete, loses data, or misled about the record or who sees it; major = slowed, confused, guessing, or a stated criterion fails on a primary screen; minor = polish.

Where a capture shows something I cannot be sure happened (for example a tap that may not have registered in automation), I say so in the issue.

## 2. Problems found

Format: screen (viewport) · what is wrong · rubric item · severity · fix. All at 390px unless stated.

### Log a UTI and the UTI (episode) page

1. **Log a UTI → Today (390px)** · With a UTI already open (23 Nov, "OPEN · DAY 2"), submitting the form again created a second open UTI (24 Nov, "OPEN · DAY 1") with no warning. History then lists two open UTIs; Today's hero says "Day 1 of this UTI" and the "UTIs, 6 months" count rose from 3 to 4; the clinician view counts it too. · 5.10, 6.7, 9.4 · **Critical** · When a UTI is open, the form should first ask "Is this the same UTI you logged on 23 Nov, or a new one?" and offer "Add to that UTI" or "It's a new UTI".
2. **Log a UTI, empty submit (logEmpty-01, log-02-submit-empty)** · Tapping "SAVE AND GO TO TODAY" with nothing chosen except the pre-selected "Today" saved a UTI with no symptoms, no triggers and no treatment ("None recorded" on every row). No validation ran. · 5.7, 6.4 · **Major** · Require at least one symptom or an explicit "Not sure / nothing yet" chip; on an empty submit show "There is a problem: choose what you are noticing, or tap Not sure yet".
3. **Log a UTI (log-01)** · "Today" is pre-selected under "When did it start?", and "Still going" is pre-selected under "Is it still going?" once Yesterday is chosen. A tired user can save without ever reading the question. · 5.6 (note the rubric's 7.5 allows "today" as a sensible default; if kept, make it visibly a default and keep the symptom question mandatory) · Minor · Leave the date unselected with "Today" first, or keep the default but show it as a quiet suggestion ("Today, change if not").
4. **Log a UTI (log-02-filled, log-03-other-long)** · After choosing a symptom chip, a "Tap to rate" row of 10 unlabelled squares appears further down. The squares are about 26px wide with about 4px between them; nothing says what 1 or 10 means; the row is revealed below the fold so the user may never see it. · 3.4, 8.4, 2.10, 8.9 · **Major** · Replace with a 0 to 10 slider or five labelled chips ("Mild", "Moderate", "Severe"), label the ends, and reveal it directly under the chip it belongs to.
5. **Log a UTI (log-03-other-long)** · The "Other" free-text box has no visible label, is a single-line input (the long entry scrolls sideways and shows "i the day and is worse when I sit for long"), and its focus ring is gold while every other field uses pink. · 5.2, 3.3, 10.8 · **Major** · Make it a labelled textarea ("Describe what else you notice (optional)") that grows with the text; use the same focus style as other inputs.
6. **Log a UTI (log-03-other-long)** · In the rating list the Other text becomes the row label ("A dull ache low down that comes and goes…"), squeezing "Tap to rate" into a three-line column. · 3.9, 3.3 · Minor · Truncate the row label to one line with the full text as a tooltip, or put the rating controls on their own line.
7. **Log a UTI (log-01)** · "What are you noticing?" is a multi-choice question with no "choose all that apply" hint and no "Not sure yet" or "None of these" option. · 5.6 · Minor · Add the hint under the question and a "Not sure yet" chip at the end.
8. **Log a UTI (log-01)** · "Same as last time" is an action styled as a plain pink text link under the question. It is useful (5.8 pre-fill) but looks like a heading or a link away. · 1.6, 10.2 · Minor · Style it as a secondary chip or button: "Use the same symptoms as last time".
