# Round 2 UX review: phone (390px)

Material: `docs/ux/screenshots/round-2/phone/` (33 static screens) and `docs/ux/screenshots/round-2/flows/phone/` (84 flow steps), all full-page captures at 390px wide. Scored cold against `docs/ux/rubric.md` with `docs/ux/product-map.md` as the description of intent. Desktop was not supplied, so heading 4 is not scored. Fixed bars appearing part-way down a full-page capture, and US-ordered native date inputs, are not counted as defects.

## 1. Score table

| Heading | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Navigation and findability | 7 | Breadcrumbs, a persistent bottom bar and task-named buttons are consistently present; let down by icon-only "..." menus, generic "Edit", "Manage", "View" and "Fill in the form" links, a UTI page whose title is only a date, and cards that do not look tappable. |
| 2. Clarity of wording | 6 | Body copy is calm plain English with good question headings, but developer keys ("water", "pee_after_sex") reach the About you form, lab notation (">10^5", "USS KUB", "PVR") and abbreviated dates ("Tue 24 Nov", "Nov · 2") appear on primary screens, and the consent text runs to 90-word paragraphs. |
| 3. Mobile layout | 7 | No sideways scroll, single column, chips and faces at or above 44px, sticky Save on the log form; repeated small text (12 to 13px hints, legends and pills), 36px S/I/R targets, several primary buttons that are not full width, and awkward wrapping when a custom symptom name is long. |
| 4. Desktop layout | not scored | No desktop material supplied. |
| 5. Forms and data entry | 6 | Log a UTI is quick (Today/Yesterday/2 days ago, chips, duplicate-UTI guard, sticky Save) and results offer "I don't know the date" and "Not sure"; but Investigations and Taking rely on a calendar-only date, "Normal" and "Standard culture" are pre-selected answers, validation shows one error at a time at the foot of the sheet, and ending a UTI never asks what worked. |
| 6. Feedback and states | 5 | Skeleton loading, inline "Saved" ticks and named confirmations for every destructive action are good; but no form has an error summary, inline errors sit at the bottom rather than at the field, one error stays after being fixed, adding a record gives no confirmation while removing does, and withdrawing consent lands on an empty page. Submitting the consent form unticked shows nothing at all. |
| 7. First-time experience | 5 | Consent is un-ticked, explains storage plainly and offers "Prefer a form?"; but its primary button is about three screens below the fold, re-consenting sends an existing user with a full record back through guided setup, "Add UTIs quickly" has no form alternative, and there is no help or contact route on any screen. |
| 8. Accessibility | 6 | Focus rings are visible and S/I/R pills carry letters, not just colour; but the 30-day grid, the 12-month grid and the "Feeling" squares are colour-only, several hints and pills are small light-grey on pink or white on a gradient, the "Antibiotics you rated" bars are near-invisible, and three icon-only buttons have no visible label. |
| 9. Trust and tone | 7 | Tone is consistently calm and factual, sharing is explicit with "opened once" visibility, and "This is your record, not advice" is everywhere; but the patient's own screens label their entries "PATIENT", "Normal" is pre-selected on a health record, Una answers "feeling fine, no symptoms" by asking when the new UTI started, and shop upsells sit inside the health record pages. |
| 10. Consistency | 6 | Buttons, chips, cards and sheets share one visual system; but date formats vary across six styles, the same kind of action is confirmed on some screens and not others ("Removed." / "Result removed." / nothing), sheets mix DONE-saves-as-you-go with SAVE/Cancel, voice flips between "my" and "your", and "test", "result", "scan" and "investigation" are used interchangeably. |

## 2. Problems found

Severity: critical, major, minor as defined in the rubric. Viewport is 390px phone throughout.

### Sign in, kit code and shared-Passport entry

1. `login`, `login-code`, `start`, `shared-passport`: no help, contact or "what is this" route anywhere on the public screens; the footer with "Your data and privacy" only appears after sign-in. Breaks 7.7, 8.12. Major. Fix: add a one-line footer ("Need help? Email hello@... " and a privacy link) under the card on every public screen, in the same place as the signed-in footer.
2. `login`: the hint "We'll email you a one-time code. No password, no account to create." sits under the field, not between the label and the field, and ends with full stops. Breaks 5.2, 2.8. Minor. Fix: move the hint under the "Email address" label as one phrase without a full stop.
3. `login`: no route from the sign-in card to "Enter a kit code" or "Open a shared Passport"; a clinician or a kit-in-hand user who lands on `/login` has nowhere to go. Breaks 1.5, 1.10. Minor. Fix: two text links under the card: "I have a kit code" and "I'm a clinician with a share code".
4. `signin-02-bad-email`: the error "Enter an email address in the right format, like name@example.com." appears under the hint with a red border, but there is no "There is a problem" summary and the browser title does not change. Breaks 6.4. Minor (single-field form). Fix: add the summary pattern even on one-field forms so it is the same everywhere, and prefix the title with "Error:".
5. `start`: the hint "Dashes and capitals don't matter." is under the field with a full stop; the footer line "You'll be asked to sign in or create an account first, then taken to your questionnaire." uses "questionnaire" where the kit page says "symptom form". Breaks 2.8, 10.5. Minor. Fix: move the hint above the field; say "then to your symptom form".
6. `start-error`: the error paragraph is 30 words across three sentences ("That doesn't look like a Utee kit code. It has 8 letters and numbers, printed under the QR code like UT-7K4M-92QX. Check each character and try again.") and there is no error summary. Breaks 2.1, 6.4. Minor. Fix: "Enter the 8-character code printed under the QR code, like UT-7K4M-92QX".
7. `share-clinician-01-wrong-code`: the error text appears but the Share code field keeps its grey border; there is no visible tie between the message and the field. Breaks 6.5. Minor. Fix: red border and hidden "Error:" prefix as on the email field.
8. All public screens: buttons are rendered in all capitals with wide letter-spacing ("CONTINUE WITH EMAIL", "OPEN PASSPORT"). Acceptable under 2.6 only if the underlying text is sentence case, which cannot be confirmed; the style also reduces legibility at 13 to 14px. Breaks 2.6 (conditionally), 3.3. Minor. Fix: sentence-case button text at 16px without tracking.

11. `portal-home`: the "From Utee" row scrolls sideways with cards cut off and no explicit cue; the "COMING SOON" pill is an urgency badge and the "Consultant urologist appointment £50" card is a sales item on the health portal home. Breaks 1.9, 9.10. Minor. Fix: stack the cards, drop "Coming soon" items until they exist, and move offers below the health content with a plain heading "Other Utee services".
12. `portal-home`: white 14px body text on the pink gradient card ("A telephone appointment to review your result...") and the translucent "COMING SOON" pill are unlikely to reach 4.5:1. Breaks 8.1. Minor. Fix: dark text on a flat light card.
13. `orders`: the order card leads with "UT1042" and the row is not visibly a link to the order detail. Breaks 1.8, 1.6. Minor. Fix: heading "Test kit, 6 November 2026", order number as secondary text, and a "See this order" link.

15. `kit`: the pill "YOUR REPORT IS READY" directly above a card headed "Your report is ready" repeats the same words twice. Breaks 1.1. Minor. Fix: drop the pill or make it "Report ready".
16. `kit`: "Track on Royal Mail →" opens an external site with no "opens in a new tab" wording. Breaks 1.6. Minor. Fix: "Track on the Royal Mail website (opens in a new tab)".
17. `kit`: the submitted-symptoms summary uses "Needing to urinate more frequently than normal", "Pain or burning sensation when you are urinating", "Episodes, past 6 months", and the raw-looking "Past 24 hours: Worse (-2)". Breaks 2.3, 10.5, 2.9. Minor. Fix: "Peeing more often than usual", "UTIs in the past 6 months", "Past 24 hours: worse".
18. `kit`: labels use trailing colons ("Had a UTI before:  Yes", "Duration:  1–3 days"). Breaks 2.8. Minor. Fix: label and value in two columns without colons.
