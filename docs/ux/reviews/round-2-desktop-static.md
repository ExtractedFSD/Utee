# Round 2 review: desktop, static screens (1440px)

Material: the 33 full-page JPEGs in `docs/ux/screenshots/round-2/desktop/` and `round-2/index.json`. Six files are byte-identical pairs (cultures = record-results, investigations = record-investigations, prevention = record-taking, about-me = record-about, timeline = history-everything, diary = history-days) because the old addresses redirect; `log-form` is identical to `log`; `consent` is identical to `today` (consent already given) and `login-code` is the email step with the address filled, not the code step. `index.json` reports no sideways scroll on any desktop screen.

## 1. Scores

| Heading | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Navigation and findability | 6 | Passport pills (Today, History, Record, Share, Una) are on every Passport page and sub-pages carry a back link, but Share is not one tap from the portal home, UTI cards, order rows and kit rows give no visible sign they open anything (the only cue is "Tap a UTI to open it." after four cards or a "RESULT READY · VIEW" pill), the top bar never marks the current page, and Settings highlights "Today". |
| 2. Clarity of wording | 6 | Most copy is short, direct and NHS-flavoured ("pee", "GP", "Choose all that apply"), but every button, tab, pill and eyebrow renders in letter-spaced capitals, lab and scan jargon reaches the screen unexplained ("USS KUB", "PVR 20 ml", ">10^5", "Worse (-2)", "worked: yes"), and "Continue", "View", "Edit", "Manage" say nothing about where they go. |

Forms and data entry | 7 (static only) | Judged from what stills show: labels sit above fields, known answers are pre-filled, start dates offer Today / Yesterday / 2 days ago / Earlier, "Prefer not to say" and "Skip for now" exist, hints say why ("If yes, we'll show safety information..."). | 8. Accessibility | 5 | Several things fail on sight: white nav labels and small white text on the light end of the pink gradient, 12px grey footnotes and chart captions, feeling squares and the 30-day heat map conveyed by colour alone, toggles with no on/off text and a near-white off state, and a 26px-tall pill as the only link to the kit page. ## 2. Problems found

### Global (every portal and Passport page unless stated)

1. All pages, desktop 1440: the top bar labels "HOME", "UTI PASSPORT", "ORDERS", "SUBSCRIPTIONS" look identical on every page; nothing marks the current section (compare Today and Orders). Breaks 1.1 and 1.4. Minor. Fix: give the current tab a filled pill or underline, with `aria-current="page"`.
2. All pages, desktop 1440: no help or contact route anywhere. The footer offers "Utee patient portal", "Your data and privacy", "Your test kits"; the public pages (Sign in, Enter kit code, Open a shared Passport) have no footer at all. Breaks 7.7, 10.7, 8.12. Major. Fix: add "Help and contact" to the footer of every page and under the card on the three public screens.
3. All pages, desktop 1440: every button, nav label, tab, eyebrow and status pill renders as letter-spaced capitals ("SAVE AND GO TO TODAY", "RESULT READY · VIEW", "YOUR PORTAL", "UTI PASSPORT"). Breaks 2.6 unless the DOM text is sentence case under a `text-transform` (cannot be confirmed from stills; see section 3). Minor. Fix: set buttons and tabs in sentence case; keep small caps, if at all, for eyebrow labels only.
4. All pages, desktop 1440: white nav labels "HOME" and "UTI PASSPORT" sit on the light pink end of the header gradient (around x 470 to 680) where white on roughly #e98cb8 is about 2.3:1. The same applies to the white Utee wordmark. Breaks 8.1. Major. Fix: darken the gradient under the nav, or set the labels in the dark plum used for body text.
5. Today, History, Record, Share, Settings, Log a UTI, desktop 1440: the Passport uses three content widths and left edges under one nav row. Today is 1104px wide starting at x168 (aligned with the pills), History is 768px at x336, Record / Log / Share / Settings / Setup are 672px at x384. The h1 therefore jumps between x168, x336 and x384 as the user moves between tabs, while the "UNA" pill stays at the far right. Breaks 4.4 and 10.6. Major. Fix: one page container (about 960px) for every Passport page, with the pills, h1 and content sharing a left edge; let the wide Today grid live inside it.
6. History (UTIs, Everything), Day by day, Guided setup, desktop 1440: the copy says "Tap a UTI to open it.", "Tap any day to add or change it." and "Tap one, or say..." to a mouse user. Breaks 4.10 and 10.10. Minor. Fix: "Choose a UTI to open it", "Choose any day to add or change it", "Choose one".
7. History (UTIs, Everything), Record · Results, Record · Investigations, desktop 1440: the source pill on the user's own entries reads "PATIENT". The record is addressed to "you" everywhere else. Breaks 9.11 and 2.3. Major. Fix: "Added by you" (and keep "Utee lab" for lab results).
8. History · Everything, Record · Results, kit page, desktop 1440: generic antibiotic names are capitalised ("Cefalexin", "Nitrofurantoin", "Trimethoprim", "Pivmecillinam", "Co-amoxiclav"). Breaks 2.6 (NHS style lower-cases generic medicines). Minor. Fix: lower-case generic names; capitalise brands only.
9. Record · About you, Share, Settings, Open a shared Passport, Guided setup, desktop 1440: "passport" is lower case in running text ("as they appear on your passport", "your passport PDF", "A copy of everything in your passport", "Delete my passport data", "opens the passport straight away", "set up your passport") while headings and the nav say "UTI Passport". Breaks 10.5. Minor. Fix: pick one ("Passport") and use it everywhere.
10. Today, Record · Taking, History · Everything, desktop 1440: dates drop the year in some places ("USS KUB · 11 May", "since 22 January", "18 November" in the left column) and keep it in others ("9 November 2026", "Ended 18 November 2026"). Breaks 10.4 and 2.5. Minor. Fix: always "11 May 2026"; a record spanning years must not rely on the reader inferring the year.
11. Record · Results, Record · Investigations, Record · Taking, Today, About you step 1, desktop 1440: the same disclaimer appears under every list ("This is your record, not advice.", "not a recommendation", "Nothing here is a recommendation." twice on one card). Breaks 9.4 and 9.5 (reassurance should be specific; repetition reads as nervousness). Minor. Fix: say it once on the consent screen and once in Settings; drop the per-list footnotes or keep one.
12. Record · Results, Record · Investigations, Record · Taking, Today ("My patterns" captions, "Order a Utee test"), kit page (tracking numbers), Enter kit code (footnote), desktop 1440: footnotes and captions are about 12 to 13px in mid grey on white or pale pink. Breaks 8.1 (and the spirit of 3.3's 14px floor). Minor. Fix: 14px minimum and the dark plum text colour.
